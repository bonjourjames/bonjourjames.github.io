Bio

DJ James Shin moves between worlds — DJ, yoga teacher, creative director. On the dancefloor, these aren't separate identities. They converge into one signal.

Based in Busan, James architects spaces of freedom through sound. Opposing energies don't collide in his sets. They balance. Light and dark, power and softness, tension and release: each element finds its place within a carefully constructed arc.

Drawing from extensive international experience and a deep affinity for Latin groove culture, his sound moves across a wide spectrum — from the outward pull of rhythm to Techno as an inner journey — beats as mantra, a room breathing in the same pulse.

A background in design and dance informs every performance. The stage isn't just a set — it's a statement. As a defining voice in Busan's club scene and a respected figure in Korea's LGBT community, James creates dancefloors that feel free, safe, and belonging.
